**Resilient multi-agent reasoning.** Agents that coordinate under uncertainty, keep memory across long contexts, and change strategy when they notice they are wrong.

**Emergent alignment in instruction-tuned models.** The implicit preferences and biases that training produces and standard benchmarks miss.

**Retrieval past naive RAG.** Knowledge graphs fused with dense vectors and tool outputs, so a pipeline can reason about what it retrieves instead of matching on surface similarity.

I read as many books as I can, as method rather than decoration. Stories carry the patterns multi-step reasoning struggles with: dependencies that span hundreds of pages, narrators you shouldn't trust, foreshadowing that works as prediction. So I build embeddings from book-level thematic analysis, and work toward agents that keep an internal story world while they act in an external one.
