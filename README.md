### `~/research`

- **Computational neuroscience.** Computational models of the brain, and what they can teach AI systems.
- **Resilient multi-agent reasoning.** Agents that coordinate under uncertainty, keep memory across long contexts, and change strategy when they are wrong.
- **Emergent alignment.** The implicit preferences and biases instruction-tuned models pick up in training that standard benchmarks miss.
- **Retrieval past naive RAG.** Knowledge graphs fused with dense vectors and tool outputs, so a pipeline can reason about what it retrieves.
